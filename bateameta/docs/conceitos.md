## Linha Zero: O Último Trem / Line Zero: Last Train

**Gênero:** Co-op survival horror / extraction roguelite (scavenge for a quota, 'the train leaves without you')

**Pitch:** A crew of up to 6 night-shift workers rides a train on a subway line that is on no map. At every stop the doors open for 3 minutes. The crew spreads out through a flickering, procedurally generated station, drags lost cargo back to the train to hit the line's quota, and dodges entities that follow simple, learnable rules. Then everyone sprints for the doors, because the train leaves on time with or without you. A player who gets left behind has to run the pitch-black tunnel to the next station and hope the Ghost Train doesn't get there first.

**Loop:** MINUTE TO MINUTE (30-90 s): sweep rooms with a battery-limited flashlight; spot loot, hazards and anomalies; react to entity tells (lights flicker + '[distant rumble]' caption = hide in an alcove; a blank-faced Commuter = keep your light on it and back away; a PA announcement = decide whether it is genuine and obey or ignore it); ping teammates; haul cargo (heavy two-handed items slow you and stop you from holding the flashlight) back to the cargo car; keep checking the door timer. PER STOP (3-4 min): hit the stop target, optionally do the station objective (restore a fuse box for bonus lights, grab the Line Token), get back before the doors close (horn at 60 s and 15 s). PER RUN (about 18-22 min): 4 stops + Terminal; during each ride between stops spend credits on train upgrades or bank them, heal, and recover lost crewmates; survive the Terminal finale; cash out against the line quota. SESSION (45-60 min): 2-3 runs with depot shopping between them. LONG TERM: Rank 1-50 unlocks gear, train upgrades, cosmetics and harder lines/modifiers; an Anomaly Registry (bestiary + lore files) to complete; daily contracts; a weekly 'Service Notice' modifier with a leaderboard; a monthly Service Pass season.

## APAGÃO: Turno da Noite / BLACKOUT: Night Shift

**Gênero:** Co-op horror extraction (scavenge, meet the quota, escape) on procedurally generated, dark, liminal facility floors. 1-4 players per crew, first-person.

**Pitch:** You and up to 3 friends work the night shift for a shady megacorp that sends crews down into its abandoned underground facility to haul out scrap and meet the quota. Every floor runs on a dying generator: the lights flicker, dim and finally cut out in a total BLACKOUT, and the things living in the dark wake up. How long do you push your luck before running for the cargo lift?

**Loop:** MINUTE TO MINUTE: sweep dark corridors with a battery-limited flashlight; press SCAN to make nearby scrap glint; grab scrap (ProximityPrompt, 4 slots, heavy two-handed items slow you and force the flashlight off, so a teammate has to light the way); read the tells (lights flicker = the Dimmer is coming, hide in a locker and kill your light; a stone-crack sound = the Mannequin moved, keep eyes on it; skittering = the Crawler, walk and crouch, don't drop things); fix fuse boxes as a pair to buy power time; run loot back to the cargo lift. SESSION (about 15-25 min): a Contract = 3 floors in a row, each about 5-6 min of power. After each floor, loot in the lift is banked toward the Contract Quota. After floor 3 you sell at the Company Terminal: meet the quota and get promoted to a deeper, harder, better-paying contract, or miss it and get 'FIRED' (contract resets, persistent rewards kept). LONG TERM: Crew Rank XP and Credits (the surplus above quota) unlock permanent tools, upgrades and code-built cosmetics; complete the Bestiary of Incident Reports; set deepest-depth records; play the Daily Seed leaderboard; new biome or entity drops every 2-3 weeks.

## Hora Extra: Turno da Noite / Overtime: Night Shift

**Gênero:** Co-op horror comedy where a squad scavenges a building and hauls loot out against a rising quota, with run-based 'contracts' (the Lethal Company / R.E.P.O. / Deadly Delivery family), set in a procedurally generated, empty-feeling ('liminal') office tower after hours.

**Pitch:** You and up to 3 friends are the night salvage crew of Lucro Infinito S.A. (Infinite Profit Inc.). Each night you ride the service elevator into a procedurally generated office tower with flickering lights. You drag fragile valuables out with real physics and hit the company's ever-rising quota before 6 AM, while faceless 'staff' hunt you. The Manager only moves when nobody is looking at him, and The Janitor hunts by sound. Three shifts make a contract. Hit the quota and you can renew for bigger pay and a longer streak. Miss it and you get a funny 'performance review' and are FIRED. It is a corporate-satire horror comedy built for squads on voice chat, clip-worthy moments and 20 to 60 minute sessions.

**Loop:** MINUTE TO MINUTE (30-90 s): creep through dark rooms with a flashlight. Scan a prop (the scanner shows its value). Decide what to grab: weight slows you, fragile items (monitors, trophies, the Golden Stapler) lose value on every hit, and heavy safes and server racks need two people or a dolly. Hear or see an entity, then choose: freeze, hide, stun it, run, or ping your crew. Haul the item to the service elevator. The quota counter ticks up with a bell sound and a screen punch, which is the dopamine hit. Near-misses and ragdoll deaths make the laugh moments.
SESSION (20-60 min): hub (Break Room) → step into a crew elevator → Shift 1 → about 60 s at the terminal to spend Company Credits on tools → Shift 2 → terminal → Shift 3 → quota check. On success the crew votes to RENEW (quota +35%, streak +1, richer loot tables and a meaner mix of entities) or CLOCK OUT and take their Paycheck. On failure they see the 'Performance Review' screen (funny stats, cause of death, 'Employee of the Month: nobody') and go back to the hub. In the hub: spend Paycheck on cosmetics, turn in daily orders, read new Bestiary entries, show off your look, and recruit people for the next contract.
LONG TERM (days and weeks): job rank ('Cargo') from Intern to CEO, which unlocks terminal tools, cosmetics and, after the MVP, new facilities (Mall at rank 10). A prestige reset called 'Aposentadoria' (Retirement) earns a badge and a nametag color. Complete the Bestiary of entities (Incident Reports with lore memos). Daily Cartão de Ponto (punch-card) streak and daily orders. A weekly 'Memo da Diretoria' (Board Memo) modifier and weekly leaderboards (Employee of the Week statue). A 'Timecard' season pass. Crew Bond levels with the same friends.

## Bate a Meta: Turno da Noite / Hit the Quota: Night Shift

**Gênero:** Co-op horror-lite extraction roguelite (Lethal Company / Doors style). 1-4 players, round-based, procedurally generated levels, fully playable solo.

**Pitch:** You are a night-shift temp for RefugoCorp S.A., a fictional and very shady salvage company. You go down into a procedurally generated, pitch-dark abandoned warehouse and office complex, find sellable junk with your flashlight and carry it back to the elevator before 06:00. Two things hunt you while you work. The Watcher is a tall, faceless figure that only moves when nobody is looking at it. The Crawler hunts by sound. Each contract has three 5-minute shifts: hit the quota and you get promoted, miss it and you are FIRED. Every screen leans on Brazilian office-life gallows humor, from the intern-to-CEO career ladder to the HR memos and Performance Reviews.

**Loop:** MINUTE TO MINUTE: You step out of the elevator into the dark with a flashlight (the battery drains). You spot scrap by its glint or with the scanner ping, then decide what to carry: 4 pockets for small items, or one heavy two-handed item that is worth more but slows you and uses both hands. Then you deal with threats: keep eyes on the Watcher, crouch or walk quietly past the Crawler, throw a flare to lure it, or stun it with the shovel. You run back, drop the loot in the elevator sell bin and see the value pop. The clock pushes you to decide between another trip deeper and getting out now. SESSION (about 20 min per contract): 3 shifts, then Quota Day. PROMOTED means bonus Credits, a bigger quota and a deeper, more dangerous facility. FIRED means an HR memo screen and a reset to contract 1. Between shifts, players spend Credits at the hub terminal on equipment. LONG TERM: career rank XP from lifetime scrap sold (Estagiário, Trainee, Júnior, Pleno, Sênior, Coordenador, Gerente, Diretor, CEO). Ranks unlock shop items and titles. Also: a scrap Catalog to complete, a best-quota global leaderboard, and a daily meal-voucher login streak. Planned for v1.1+: a daily seeded contract (the same layout for everyone that day) with its own leaderboard, new facility themes, and a new monster each month.
